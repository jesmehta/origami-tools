Origami Tools

Small tools for origami/folding-based design and fabrication.

V-Pleats based on a curve

Turn a hand-drawn curve into a flat, laser/CNC-ready V-pleat fold pattern.

V Pleats · Linear

V pleats by mirroring cross folds across vertical folds.

V Pleats · Radial

V pleats by mirroring cross folds across radial folds around a centre.

Hypar Generator

Any convex polygon given concentric offset contours plus corner-to-centre diagonals to create hyperbolic-paraboloids.

X Span Generator

An X-span grid generator, allowing parameter variations in regular and irregular grids.
